# Plug and Play Editor — Role Notes

**Editor**: Dr Simon Wang, Language Centre, HKBU
**Platform**: STiLE (stile.hk) — OJS 3.3
**Section**: Plug and Play

## Editor Responsibilities (to clarify with STiLE team)

- [ ] Reviewing Plug and Play submissions for pedagogical quality
- [ ] Soliciting contributions from language educators
- [ ] Setting thematic focus areas for upcoming issues
- [ ] Liaising with authors on revisions
- [ ] Potentially: curating AI-assisted interactive versions of accepted articles

## Strategic Directions

### 1. SmartTextbook as Editorial Tool
Use SmartTextbook's PITA pipeline to:
- Generate interactive drafts from submitted articles (to show authors what's possible)
- Create supplementary interactive versions alongside traditional Plug and Play entries
- Offer authors a one-click "interactivise your article" tool powered by SmartTextbook

### 2. AI-Assisted Plug and Play
Propose a new submission category: **Plug and Play Interactive** — articles that come
with a SmartTextbook-style interactive module as standard, not as an optional add-on.

### 3. Cross-platform Research
Use xAPI data from SmartTextbook-hosted versions of Plug and Play articles to generate
learning analytics data for STiLE research publications.

## Connection to IEEE ProComm Work

Both STiLE Plug and Play and IEEE ProComm Interactive share the same core model:
- Static expert content → AI-assisted interactive transformation
- Open access / OER ethos
- Practitioner-researcher community
- xAPI tracking for learning analytics

The SmartTextbook platform serves both simultaneously with minimal adaptation.
The main difference: STILE is language education; IEEE ProComm is professional communication.
Both are communities where Dr Simon Wang has editorial standing.
