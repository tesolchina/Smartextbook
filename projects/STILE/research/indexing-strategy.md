## Priority Roadmap

### 🟢 Priority 1 — DOAJ (Apply NOW — STiLE likely qualifies already)

**What it is**: Directory of Open Access Journals — the gold standard OA quality seal.
Listed journals are recognized by funders, libraries, and other indexes (Scopus and WoS
both consider DOAJ listing as a positive signal).

**STiLE meets the requirements:**
- ✓ Open access — all content freely available, no embargo
- ✓ Publishing history >1 year (March 2023 → Vol. 4 in 2025)
- ✓ >10 articles published (29 confirmed)
- ✓ Peer-reviewed
- ✓ DOIs on all articles (CrossRef registered)
- ✓ ISSN registered (3005-5253)
- ✓ Individual article URLs
- ✓ OJS platform (DOAJ has deep OJS integration)
- ⚠️ **Check**: CC license must be explicitly stated on each article page
- ⚠️ **Check**: Open access statement on journal homepage

**How to apply**: https://doaj.org/apply/
**Timeline**: Decision within 3–6 months of application
**Impact**: Immediately boosts discoverability; required by many funders for OA compliance

---

### 🟢 Priority 2 — ERIC (Apply within 6 months)

**What it is**: Education Resources Information Center — US DoE database, the most
important education-specific index globally. Extremely relevant for STiLE's scope.

**Requirements:**
- ✓ Education focus — STiLE is 100% language education
- ✓ English content (majority of articles)
- ✓ Peer review process
- ✓ Regular publication schedule
- ✓ >3 years publishing history — by late 2026, STiLE will have 3+ years
- ✓ DOIs registered

**How to apply**: https://eric.ed.gov/?q=journal+application
**Timeline**: 6–12 months review
**Strategic note**: ERIC is the natural first-choice index for a language education
journal. Many authors in this field specifically check ERIC coverage before submitting.

---

### 🟡 Priority 3 — ESCI / Web of Science (Target: 2027)

**What it is**: Emerging Sources Citation Index — the entry point to Web of Science,
Clarivate's flagship database. Being in ESCI does NOT give an Impact Factor, but it
builds the citation record needed for eventual SSCI/SCI consideration.

**Requirements:**
- ✓ Peer-reviewed
- ✓ Regular publication (quarterly or better)
- ✓ DOIs on all articles
- ✓ English titles and abstracts (STiLE has some Chinese-only articles — needs work)
- ✓ Ethical publishing standards (COPE compliance helpful)
- ⚠️ Citation record — WoS looks at whether articles are cited in other WoS journals
- ⚠️ Editor/author international diversity — currently very HK-centric

**How to apply**: https://clarivate.com/webofsciencegroup/journal-evaluation/
**Timeline**: 12–18 months review
**To prepare**: Ensure all articles have English titles + abstracts. Grow author pool
beyond HK (mainland China, UK, Australia, US contributors strengthen the case).

---

### 🟡 Priority 4 — Scopus (Target: 2028)

**What it is**: Elsevier's abstract and citation database — 25,000+ journals,
the world's largest. An Impact Factor equivalent (CiteScore) is assigned to indexed
journals. Being in Scopus is a major prestige marker for Asian academics.

**Requirements (SNIP/SJR metrics after listing):**
- Peer review — explicit policy required
- Regular publication — consistent schedule
- ISSN — ✓
- DOIs — ✓
- English titles, abstracts, keywords — for ALL articles
- Cited in Scopus-indexed journals — needs 2-3 years of citation accumulation
- Editorial board diversity — international spread expected
- Ethics statement (COPE)

**How to apply**: https://suggestor.step.scopus.com/ (Scopus Source Suggestion Tool)
**Timeline**: 18–24 months review; re-apply if rejected after 1 year
**Realistic assessment**: Achievable by 2028–2029 if DOAJ + ERIC are secured first
and citation record grows.

---

### 🔴 Priority 5 — SSCI (Long-term aspiration: 2030+)

**What it is**: Social Sciences Citation Index — top tier of Web of Science, gives
full Impact Factor. Only ~4,000 journals worldwide. Extremely selective.

**Honest assessment**: STiLE would need:
- 3–5 years in ESCI first
- Consistent citation by SSCI-indexed journals
- Strong Impact Factor relative to field
- Truly international editorial board and author pool

**This is a 5–10 year goal.** DOAJ → ERIC → ESCI → Scopus → SSCI is the realistic path.

## What Dr Simon Wang Can Push For as Editor

As an incoming Plug and Play editor, these are the specific improvements that would
directly strengthen the indexing applications:

### Immediate actions (as editor)
1. **CC license on every article** — required for DOAJ. Confirm each article displays
   `CC BY 4.0` or similar in the footer/header.
2. **Open access statement** on homepage — one clear paragraph stating the journal's
   OA policy. DOAJ requires this explicitly.
3. **English abstracts on ALL articles** — the Chinese-language articles (邏輯重音,
   面向粵語母語者, 利用定制化...) need English abstracts added for ESCI/Scopus.
4. **COPE membership** — Committee on Publication Ethics. Annual fee ~£150.
   Membership is a positive signal for all major indexes.

### Structural advocacy (editorial board level)
5. **Machine-readable access** — push for the OJS REST API to be opened
   (currently returns 403). This enables third-party indexers and tools like SmartTextbook.
6. **OAI-PMH metadata quality** — the OAI feed currently returns blank titles/abstracts
   for most articles. OJS can populate these automatically from the submission metadata.
   This is a settings/workflow fix, not a technical overhaul.
7. **Author diversity** — actively invite submissions from outside HK: mainland China,
   Taiwan, Singapore, UK, Australia, North America. ESCI/Scopus reviewers look at this.
8. **Increase publication frequency** — currently publishing 1-2 issues/year with ~7-10
   articles each. Aim for consistent quarterly schedule.
